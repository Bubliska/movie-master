Opening the Mac beta.

This beta is ad-hoc signed and has not been notarized by Apple. On first launch, macOS may show “Apple could not verify Movie Master.app is free of malware.” Apple has not verified this build; the warning does not by itself identify malware.

If you trust the copy downloaded from this site or our GitHub release, Apple's app-specific opening procedure is:

  1. Open the DMG, drag Movie Master.app to Applications, and try opening it once. Dismiss the verification warning.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security. Scroll to Security and click Open Anyway beside Movie Master.
  3. Confirm that you want to open Movie Master and authenticate if macOS asks. macOS remembers the exception for this app.

Read Apple's explanation and instructions ↗. These steps apply to the verification warning above. If macOS instead says the app will damage your computer or is damaged, stop and contact the project through GitHub.

The release.

Movie Master v0.4.4 uses the .SEN document extension and opens drafts from previous versions. Scene counts, numbering and number locking are included. Numbered documents use SEN format version 2; this release also opens version 1 files. Verify the SHA-256 checksums.

Mac: Apple Silicon and macOS 15 or later. DOSBox-X is included with its licenses. The personal build is ad-hoc signed and is not Apple-notarized. DOS: 386+, VGA and extended memory, with 32 MB recommended. Windows: 32- and 64-bit builds are included; they have been compiled but not tested on a live Windows machine. Sources and third-party license notices are inside the download packages.

The app works offline. It supports Fountain import, PDF/TXT/Fountain export, Latin text and selectable keyboard layouts. Advanced revision tracking, production scheduling and online co-editing are outside its scope. App menus are in English.
